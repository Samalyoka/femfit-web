FAQ

Can I cancel my membership anytime?
Yes, you can cancel at any time. Your membership remains active until the end of the billing period.
Is there a joining fee?
No joining fee. You only pay the monthly membership price.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, Premium and Elite members can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per year.
How do I book a personal session?
After signing up, go to your profile and select a trainer. You can book sessions directly from the schedule page.
