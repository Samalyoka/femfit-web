What your trainer does for you

01
Gets to know you
Your trainer begins with a full assessment of your current fitness level, health history, goals and schedule.
02
Builds your personal programme
Based on your assessment, your trainer creates a complete training plan covering workouts, nutrition, equipment and schedule.
03
Tracks your progress
Your trainer monitors your results and adjusts the programme as you improve.
04
Supports you along the way
If something isn't working, request a revision. Your trainer will update your plan at any time.

What makes a FemFit trainer different

Women-only specialists
All our trainers are experienced in women's physiology, hormonal considerations and strength training for every stage of life.
Fully certified
Every trainer holds recognised fitness certifications and undergoes ongoing professional development.
Results-focused
Your trainer is invested in your progress — measuring outcomes, celebrating milestones and keeping you accountable.
Flexible and adaptable
Your trainer adapts your plan if your schedule shifts, your goals evolve, or you simply need something different.
